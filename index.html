Common questions

Before You Appoint a PMC

Who pays the PMC on a society redevelopment?

The society appoints the PMC. Fees are set out in the engagement letter. See the fees page for the published schedule.

Can you act for the society and the developer on the same project?

No. One client, one mandate. We do not advise the other side of that same project.

Do you handle deemed conveyance under MOFA?

Yes. Revenue-record checks, Competent Authority proceedings under Section 11, and the registered conveyance deed.

Which offices do you cover?

Mira-Bhayandar (MBMC), Thane Municipal Corporation and the wider Mumbai Metropolitan Region.
