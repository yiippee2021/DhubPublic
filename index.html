How We Work

Our Engagement Process

  • Consultation & Documentation Review

    We review your society's existing title documents, conveyance status and any prior correspondence with the competent authority or developer.

  • Strategy & Proposal

    A clear, milestone-based proposal is presented to the Managing Committee, outlining scope, timelines and fees before any general body approval is sought.

  • Execution & Filing

    We prepare and file the requisite applications, agreements and representations, and represent the society before the relevant authorities.

  • Monitoring & Reporting

    Regular status updates are shared with the Managing Committee until the matter reaches its conclusion — be it a deemed conveyance order, redevelopment agreement or OC/CC.
