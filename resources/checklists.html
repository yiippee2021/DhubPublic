Checklists

Updated for the MCS (Amendment) Rules, 2026 — not legal advice, and not a substitute for a document-specific review of your Society's records.

Please read before use: These checklists describe the general steps and documents typically involved. They do not reflect the specific status of your Society's records. For a checklist reviewed against your Society's actual file, use the Society Help Desk.

1. What's New — MCS (Amendment) Rules, 2026

Notification No. Sanini 0321/C.R.41/13C, dated 18 June 2026, inserted a new Chapter XI-B (Rules 106C-1 to 106C-14) into the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Rules, 1961, creating a dedicated governance code for Co-operative Housing Societies, Associations of Societies and Co-operative Housing Associations. Four changes matter most in practice:

  • New entity types: "Co-operative Housing Association" (2+ societies/legal bodies on one layout) and "Association of Societies" (5+ societies) are now recognised, each registered via Form Y-2 (fee Rs. 5,000).
  • Membership modernised: Rule 8 now recognises Joint and Provisional members alongside Associate members. Associate membership uses new Form Y-5A; succession uses Form Y-4 (provisional membership) or Form Y-5 (family arrangement).
  • Governance strengthened: Mandatory Model Bye-laws (adoptable within 3 months of publication), hybrid (video-conferencing) participation in General Body meetings, and enhanced redevelopment safeguards (see Section 4 below).
  • Faster dues recovery: A dedicated summary procedure under Section 154B-29, using Form Y-6 (application) and Form Y-7 (recovery certificate), generally decided within 3 months of the first hearing.

Forms J-1, J-2 and M-1 to M-19 stand withdrawn for housing societies; a new "Y-series" (Y-1 to Y-7, including Y-5A) replaces them. Managing Committees should re-map existing compliance checklists against Rules 106C-1 to 106C-14 rather than relying on the older rule numbers.

2. 2026 Amendment — Society Action Checklist

  • Review the Registrar's Model Bye-laws on publication, and adopt within the 3-month window where beneficial
  • Update the membership register and bye-laws to reflect Joint and Provisional member categories
  • Re-align the Sinking Fund (min. 0.25% p.a. of construction cost per flat) and Repair & Maintenance Fund (min. 0.75% p.a.)
  • Revise the maintenance-bill format to the prescribed apportionment basis for each head of charge (see Rule 106C-12)
  • Brief the Managing Committee on the enhanced redevelopment notice (14 days), quorum (2/3rd) and video-recording requirements
  • Train office bearers on the Section 154B-29 recovery procedure and Forms Y-6 / Y-7
  • Retire obsolete M-series and J-1/J-2 forms; transition documentation to the new Y-series (Y-1 to Y-7)
  • Confirm annual contribution to the Co-operative Education & Training Fund (Rs. 10/member/month for housing societies)

3. Deemed Conveyance — Document Checklist

  • Society registration certificate
  • Agreement for sale / allotment letters of members (as available)
  • 7/12 extract and property card of the land
  • Sanctioned building plan and Occupation Certificate
  • Promoter's/developer's title documents to the land
  • Correspondence with the promoter regarding conveyance, if any
  • List of flat owners with share certificate details
  • General Body resolution authorising the deemed conveyance application
  • Draft application in the prescribed form for the Competent Authority

4. Section 79A & Rule 106C-13 — Redevelopment Tendering Checklist

Section 79A of the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 empowers the State Government to issue directives to Societies on redevelopment tendering, and Rule 106C-13(3) (as inserted by the 2026 Amendment) now sets out specific safeguards for the redevelopment Special General Body Meeting itself. The steps below are the general sequence typically followed.

  • General Body resolution authorising the Managing Committee to initiate the redevelopment tender process
  • Appointment of a Project Management Consultant / Architect to prepare the feasibility report
  • Preparation and publication of the tender notice, inviting eligible developers
  • Pre-bid queries and site visit, if applicable
  • Receipt of technical and financial bids by the specified deadline
  • Evaluation of bids and preparation of a comparative statement
  • Presentation of the comparative statement to the General Body
  • Special General Body meeting: 14 clear days' notice, quorum of two-thirds of total members, held in the presence of the Registrar's representative, and video recorded (Rule 106C-13(3)(i))
  • Resolution approving the selected developer: 51% majority of total members, including those on video conferencing (Rule 106C-13(3)(j))
  • Execution of the Development Agreement and Power of Attorney

5. General Redevelopment Process Checklist

  • Structural audit confirming redevelopment eligibility
  • Feasibility study — FSI/TDR computation and corpus estimation
  • Decision on self-redevelopment or developer-led redevelopment
  • Tender process and developer/contractor selection (see Section 4 above)
  • Development Agreement, Permanent Alternate Accommodation Agreement and Power of Attorney execution
  • Statutory approvals — plan sanction, Commencement Certificate
  • Member relocation and rent/corpus disbursement as agreed
  • Construction monitoring through to Occupation/Completion Certificate
  • Conveyance/lease documentation on handover
