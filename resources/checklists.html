Checklists

Updated for the MCS (Amendment) Rules, 2026 — not legal advice, and not a substitute for a document-specific review of your Society's records. Last reviewed: 16 October 2026.

Please read before use: These checklists describe the general steps and documents typically involved. They do not reflect the specific status of your Society's records. For a checklist reviewed against your Society's actual file, use the Society Help Desk.

1. What's New — MCS (Amendment) Rules, 2026

Notification No. Sanini 0321/C.R.41/13C, dated 18 June 2026, inserted a new Chapter XI-B (Rules 106C-1 to 106C-14) into the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Rules, 1961, creating a dedicated governance code for Co-operative Housing Societies, Associations of Societies and Co-operative Housing Associations. Four changes matter most in practice:

  • New entity types: "Co-operative Housing Association" (2+ societies/legal bodies on one layout) and "Association of Societies" (5+ societies) are now recognised, each registered via Form Y-2 (fee Rs. 5,000).
  • Membership modernised: Rule 8 now recognises Joint and Provisional members alongside Associate members. Associate membership uses new Form Y-5A; succession uses Form Y-4 (provisional membership) or Form Y-5 (family arrangement).
  • Governance strengthened: Mandatory Model Bye-laws (adoptable within 3 months of publication), hybrid (video-conferencing) participation in General Body meetings, and enhanced redevelopment safeguards (see Section 4 below).
  • Faster dues recovery: A dedicated summary procedure under Section 154B-29, using Form Y-6 (application) and Form Y-7 (recovery certificate), generally decided within 3 months of the first hearing.

Forms J-1, J-2 and M-1 to M-19 stand withdrawn for housing societies; a new "Y-series" (Y-1 to Y-7, including Y-5A) replaces them. Managing Committees should re-map existing compliance checklists against Rules 106C-1 to 106C-14 rather than relying on the older rule numbers.

2. 2026 Amendment — Society Action Checklist

  • Review the Registrar's Model Bye-laws on publication, and adopt within the 3-month window where beneficial
  • Update the membership register and bye-laws to reflect Joint and Provisional member categories
  • Re-align the Sinking Fund (min. 0.25% p.a. of construction cost per flat) and Repair & Maintenance Fund (min. 0.75% p.a.)
  • Revise the maintenance-bill format to the prescribed apportionment basis for each head of charge (see Rule 106C-12)
  • Brief the Managing Committee on the enhanced redevelopment notice (14 days), quorum (2/3rd) and video-recording requirements
  • Train office bearers on the Section 154B-29 recovery procedure and Forms Y-6 / Y-7
  • Retire obsolete M-series and J-1/J-2 forms; transition documentation to the new Y-series (Y-1 to Y-7)
  • Confirm annual contribution to the Co-operative Education & Training Fund (Rs. 10/member/month for housing societies)

3. Deemed Conveyance — Document Checklist

  • Society registration certificate
  • Agreement for sale / allotment letters of members (as available)
  • 7/12 extract and property card of the land
  • Sanctioned building plan and Occupation Certificate
  • Promoter's/developer's title documents to the land
  • Correspondence with the promoter regarding conveyance, if any
  • List of flat owners with share certificate details
  • General Body resolution authorising the deemed conveyance application
  • Draft application in the prescribed form for the Competent Authority

4. Redevelopment Procedure Checklist — GR dated 30 September 2026

This checklist follows Government Resolution No. सगृयो-2026/प्र.क्र.108/14-स dated 30 September 2026, issued under Section 79A of the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960. It supersedes the GR dated 4 July 2019. Rule references are to the MCS (Amendment) Rules, 2026. This is a process checklist, not legal advice.

A. Initiation and General Body approval

  • Requisition from at least one-fifth of the society's total members, with plans and suggestions, to the Secretary; the committee notes it within 8 days
  • Special General Body Meeting convened within 2 months; agenda to every member at least 14 days in advance
  • Members' written suggestions accepted up to 8 days before the meeting
  • Quorum: two-thirds of total members. If not met, adjourn and reconvene within a month on 7 days' notice; if still not met, the meeting is cancelled and the matter cannot return to an SGM for 3 months
  • At least 51% of total members physically present; video-conferencing members count only towards quorum and majority beyond that physical minimum
  • Resolution passed by a majority of members present, with approval of at least 51% of total members

B. Architect or PMC

  • Three quotations from architects or project management consultants on the government or local-authority panel
  • SGM selects one expert; appointment letter within 30 days; written agreement on the terms approved by the SGM
  • Survey of building and land; FSI/TDR and DCPR/UDCPR options; members' suggestions considered
  • Project Report prepared within 2 months of appointment
  • Committee meeting within 30 days of receiving the Project Report; members notified at least 7 days before the next SGM

C. Tender

  • Draft tender finalised at the next SGM and published in local Marathi and English newspapers
  • Minimum three valid tenders; if fewer, the tender period is extended by 15 days, then by a further 7 days
  • Secretary posts the list of tenders on the notice board; SGM convened within 30 days of the last tender date
  • Tenderers or their authorised representatives may attend the tender opening

D. Developer selection SGM

  • Proposal to the Registrar (minutes, member list, Project Report, tender details and comparison); the Registrar decides within 14 days to appoint an Authorised Officer
  • SGM convened within one month of that appointment; agenda and comparative statement to every member 14 days in advance, by hand, post or e-mail, with proof of delivery
  • Held in the presence of the Registrar's representative; the Authorised Officer verifies attendance and quorum
  • Developer selected by a majority of members (not less than 51% of total members); hand vote, or secret ballot if one-fifth of members demand it in writing
  • Written consent of at least 51% of total members for the appointment
  • Video recording; one copy with the Secretary and one with the Registrar
  • Within 14 days: minutes, members' written consent letters, the developer's consent letter, the attendance sheet, the Authorised Officer's report and the video, filed with the Registrar

E. Development Agreement and allotment

  • Agreement signed within 3 months of the SGM, on the terms approved by the SGM
  • Completion period counted from the plinth certificate: 2 years maximum, and 3 years only in exceptional cases
  • Bank guarantee amount and form approved by the General Body; alternate accommodation, rent and deposit, or transit arrangements in the agreement
  • Agreement registered under the Registration Act, 1908; each member's PAAA registered within 3 months of the agreement's registration
  • RERA carpet area stated; development rights non-transferable; dispute clause (competent court or authority under the prevailing law)
  • Members vacate only after all legal approvals and registration of their PAAA
  • New flat-holders: membership and share certificate decided within 3 months of application
  • Flat allotment by floor order or draw, with General Body approval (not less than 51% of total members), video recorded, and the list filed with the Registrar within one month
  • No committee member or office-bearer may be the developer, or a relative of the developer
  • Sanctioned building plans placed before the General Body

F. Federation and cluster redevelopment

  • Through a federation that holds the land rights: federation SGM quorum of two-thirds of affiliated societies, with approval as above
  • Each affiliated society: its own SGM with two-thirds quorum and 51% approval, with written consents filed with the federation; or 60% of all affiliated members combined

G. Records and calculations

  • Any fraction in a quorum or majority calculation is rounded up to the next whole number
  • Members may inspect documents free of charge; certified copies on payment of a prescribed fee
  • A breach may make responsible office-bearers and committee members liable under Section 79A(3) read with Section 78A

5. General Redevelopment Process Checklist

  • Structural audit confirming redevelopment eligibility
  • Feasibility study — FSI/TDR computation and corpus estimation
  • Decision on self-redevelopment or developer-led redevelopment
  • Tender process and developer/contractor selection (see Section 4 above)
  • Development Agreement, Permanent Alternate Accommodation Agreement and Power of Attorney execution
  • Statutory approvals — plan sanction, Commencement Certificate
  • Member relocation and rent/corpus disbursement as agreed
  • Construction monitoring through to Occupation/Completion Certificate
  • Conveyance/lease documentation on handover
