Sample Notices & Tender Model

Downloadable, illustrative formats — updated for the MCS (Amendment) Rules, 2026. Not legal advice, and not a substitute for review against your Society's current bye-laws.

Updated for the MCS (Amendment) Rules, 2026: Notification No. Sanini 0321/C.R.41/13C, dated 18 June 2026, inserted a new Chapter XI-B (Rules 106C-1 to 106C-14) into the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Rules, 1961, creating a dedicated governance code for housing societies, with a new "Y-series" of forms (Y-1 to Y-7) replacing the earlier J-1, J-2 and M-1 to M-19 forms. The notices below reflect this Chapter. See the Checklists page for a fuller summary.
Please read before use: These are illustrative structures only, to show what such a notice typically contains. They are not drawn from your Society's records and must be checked against your Society's current bye-laws before issue. Each notice below is also available as an editable Word (.docx) download. For a reviewed or customised version, use the Society Help Desk.

1. Sample AGM Notice

An Annual General Meeting (AGM) notice is issued by the Secretary, on behalf of the Managing Committee. Under Rule 106C-13(3)(e) of the MCS Rules, 1961 (as inserted by the 2026 Amendment), quorum for the AGM is two-thirds of the total members, or twenty members, whichever is less, and members may now participate in person or via video conferencing where the Society provides that facility.

⇩ Download Sample AGM Notice (.docx)

Illustrative structure

  1. Society name, registration number and registered address (letterhead)
  2. Notice heading: "Notice of Annual General Meeting"
  3. Date, day, time and venue of the meeting
  4. Agenda items, typically including:
    • Reading and confirmation of the previous AGM's minutes
    • Consideration and adoption of the audited accounts for the financial year
    • Appointment or re-appointment of the statutory auditor
    • Election of the Managing Committee (if due)
    • Status update on redevelopment or deemed conveyance, if applicable
    • Any other business with the Chairman's permission
  5. Note on quorum, and the provision for an adjourned meeting if quorum is not met
  6. Date of issue, and signature block for the Secretary/Chairman

2. Sample Redevelopment SGM Notice

A Special General Body Meeting (SGM) for redevelopment carries enhanced safeguards under Rule 106C-13(3)(i)–(j) of the MCS Rules, 1961 (2026 Amendment): a mandatory 14 clear days' notice (against 5 days for an ordinary SGM), quorum raised to two-thirds of total members, attendance by a representative of the Registrar who files a factual report, mandatory video recording of the meeting (lodged with the Assistant/Deputy Registrar), and developer/contractor selection requiring a 51% majority of total members, including those on video conferencing.

⇩ Download Sample Redevelopment SGM Notice (.docx)

Illustrative structure

  1. Society name, registration number and registered address (letterhead)
  2. Notice heading: "Notice of Special General Body Meeting" — issued at least 14 clear days in advance
  3. Date, day, time and venue of the meeting
  4. A clearly worded single-purpose agenda — for example: "To consider and approve the appointment of M/s [Developer Name] for redevelopment of the Society's property, on the terms placed before the General Body"
  5. A note confirming the presence of the Registrar's representative and that the meeting will be video recorded
  6. The majority required to pass the resolution (51% of total members, including those on VC)
  7. Date of issue, and signature block for the Secretary/Chairman

3. Sample Public Notice Inviting Legal Heirs

Where a member dies without a nomination and no nominee comes forward, Rule 106C-6(1)(b) requires the Society to invite legal heirs to apply for provisional membership (Form Y-4), by publishing a notice in at least two local daily newspapers of wide circulation and on the Society's notice board.

4. Sample Committee Vacancy Notice

To fill a casual vacancy in the Managing Committee, Rule 106C-13(4)(b) requires the Secretary to invite nominations from members via a 7-day notice on the Society's notice board, before the Committee fills the seat by majority vote if nominations exceed vacancies.

5. Tender Document Model (Developer Selection)

Where a Society floats a tender to select a developer for redevelopment, the tender document typically sets out eligibility, technical and financial bid requirements, and the evaluation process.

Illustrative structure

  1. Invitation to tender — brief description of the property, plot area and redevelopment intent
  2. Eligibility criteria — minimum years in business, past project experience, net worth/turnover thresholds, registration under MahaRERA
  3. Technical bid requirements — proposed FSI/TDR utilisation, layout concept, construction timeline, technical team credentials
  4. Financial bid format — corpus payment, rent during construction, additional carpet area per member, security deposit/bank guarantee, a clear comparison table format across bidders
  5. Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) amount and forfeiture conditions
  6. Evaluation criteria and weightage between technical and financial components
  7. Submission deadline, mode of submission, and validity period of the offer
  8. Disclaimer reserving the Society's right to reject any or all tenders without assigning reasons

6. Custom Resolution Drafting

The sample notices above are general formats for the notice calling a meeting. The resolution itself — the specific operative text a General Body actually votes on (developer appointment, bye-law amendment, corpus approval, and similar) — needs to be drafted against your Society's specific facts, current bye-laws and the applicable Rules, and is not something a generic template can safely cover.
