Sample Notices & Tender Model

Illustrative formats for reference only — not legal advice, and not a substitute for review against your Society's current bye-laws.

Please read before use: These are illustrative structures only, to show what such a notice or document typically contains. They are not drawn from your Society's records, do not cite specific Government Resolution numbers or dates, and must be checked against your Society's current bye-laws and any applicable circular before issue. For a reviewed or customised version, use the Society Help Desk.

1. Sample AGM Notice

An Annual General Meeting (AGM) notice is typically issued by the Secretary, on behalf of the Managing Committee, at least 14 clear days before the meeting (verify the notice period currently applicable under your Society's bye-laws).

Illustrative structure

  1. Society name, registration number and registered address (letterhead)
  2. Notice heading: "Notice of Annual General Meeting"
  3. Date, day, time and venue of the meeting
  4. Agenda items, typically including:
    • Reading and confirmation of the previous AGM's minutes
    • Consideration and adoption of the audited accounts for the financial year
    • Appointment or re-appointment of the statutory auditor
    • Election of the Managing Committee (if due)
    • Status update on redevelopment or deemed conveyance, if applicable
    • Any other business with the Chairman's permission
  5. Note on quorum, and the provision for an adjourned meeting if quorum is not met
  6. Date of issue, and signature block for the Secretary/Chairman

2. Sample GBM / SGM Notice

A Special General Body Meeting (SGM), sometimes referred to as a General Body Meeting (GBM) called for a specific purpose, is typically convened to approve a specific resolution outside the AGM cycle — for example, developer appointment for redevelopment, corpus fund distribution, or a bye-law amendment.

Illustrative structure

  1. Society name, registration number and registered address (letterhead)
  2. Notice heading: "Notice of Special General Body Meeting"
  3. Date, day, time and venue of the meeting
  4. A clearly worded single-purpose agenda — for example: "To consider and approve the appointment of M/s [Developer Name] for redevelopment of the Society's property, on the terms placed before the General Body"
  5. Reference to the relevant enabling provision under the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 / Model Bye-laws, and any applicable Section 79A directive on redevelopment tendering (verify the current, applicable reference before citing it)
  6. Note on the special majority required for the resolution, if any
  7. Date of issue, and signature block for the Secretary/Chairman

3. Tender Document Model (Developer Selection)

Where a Society floats a tender to select a developer for redevelopment, the tender document typically sets out eligibility, technical and financial bid requirements, and the evaluation process.

Illustrative structure

  1. Invitation to tender — brief description of the property, plot area and redevelopment intent
  2. Eligibility criteria — minimum years in business, past project experience, net worth/turnover thresholds, registration under MahaRERA
  3. Technical bid requirements — proposed FSI/TDR utilisation, layout concept, construction timeline, technical team credentials
  4. Financial bid format — corpus payment, rent during construction, additional carpet area per member, security deposit/bank guarantee, a clear comparison table format across bidders
  5. Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) amount and forfeiture conditions
  6. Evaluation criteria and weightage between technical and financial components
  7. Submission deadline, mode of submission, and validity period of the offer
  8. Disclaimer reserving the Society's right to reject any or all tenders without assigning reasons
