For Co-operative Housing Societies

PMC & Redevelopment Services for Housing Societies

D-HUB Group provides end-to-end Project Management Consultancy for Co-operative Housing Societies undertaking redevelopment, deemed conveyance or statutory compliance in Maharashtra. On every such engagement, we are appointed by the Society, and our mandate runs exclusively to the Society and its members. If you are a builder, developer or institution, see our PMC & design consultancy services instead.

01

Redevelopment Feasibility Study

FSI/TDR computation, carpet-area projections, corpus fund estimation, development-potential assessment, and self-redevelopment versus builder-led comparative analysis.

02

Tender Design & Developer Evaluation

Professional tender documentation, evaluation criteria, developer shortlisting, background verification, and comparative bid analysis to support Managing Committee decision-making.

03

Deemed Conveyance under MOFA

Revenue record verification, Competent Authority proceedings under MOFA Section 11, and registered conveyance deed execution for Societies where the promoter has not conveyed title.

  • Title and chain-of-documents verification
  • Drafting and filing applications before the Competent Authority (District Deputy Registrar)
  • Representation at Competent Authority hearings
  • Liaison for stamping and registration of the deemed conveyance order
  • Advisory on unilateral deemed conveyance where the promoter is untraceable or non-cooperative
04

Self-Redevelopment Advisory

End-to-end guidance covering financing structures, bank liaison, PMC representation, contractor empanelment, and compliance management for self-redevelopment models.

05

Architectural & Commencement Certificate Facilitation

Plan sanctions, FSI computations, layout approvals, and Commencement Certificate (CC) applications under MBMC, TMC and BMC jurisdictions.

06

Structural Audit & Assessment

Independent structural audits for redevelopment eligibility, building age certification, and technical reports for Managing Committee and statutory authority submissions.

07

MahaRERA & Regulatory Compliance

MahaRERA registration support, complaint advisory, recovery application assistance, and ongoing compliance monitoring under MOFA, the MCS Act, DCPR 2034 and UDCPR 2020.

08

Legal Scrutiny & Agreement Review

Developer agreement vetting, redevelopment deed review, deemed conveyance petition drafting, Society bye-law compliance, and housing litigation support.

09

Revenue Record & Title Resolution

7/12 discrepancy resolution, Urban Land Ceiling (ULC) advisory, title search, land-area enhancement coordination, and legacy title-issue management for complex schemes.

10

Society Stakeholder Management

Member communication, General Body presentation design, Managing Committee advisory, dispute de-escalation, and transparent milestone documentation.

11

Second Opinion Consultancy

An independent review where a Society, Managing Committee or individual member already has a consultant, architect, PMC or advocate engaged, but wants an outside professional opinion before relying on it — for example, on a feasibility study, a redevelopment strategy, or the merits and progress of a pending court or MahaRERA matter. This is available to Societies and to individual members, separately from any existing engagement.

Fee: Approximately Rs. 10,000, depending on the number of consultation sessions and the complexity of the matter — confirmed with you before work begins.

Regulatory Expertise

Frameworks We Practise Under

The D-HUB team is conversant with, and practises actively under, the following statutory and regulatory frameworks.

DCPR 2034 (BMC jurisdiction) UDCPR 2020 (MBMC / TMC) MahaRERA Act, 2016 MOFA, 1963 MCS Act, 1960 ULC Act, 1976 (Advisory) Revenue Records (7/12) MahaRERA Recovery (Section 40)
Our mandate on Society engagements: When appointed as PMC by a Co-operative Housing Society, D-HUB Group acts exclusively for that Society and its members, with no advisory or financial engagement with the developer on the same redevelopment. This is separate from our PMC and design consultancy work for builders, developers and institutions, which are distinct engagements on different projects.
Please note: Statutory provisions, Government Resolutions and procedural requirements referred to above are subject to periodic amendment. D-HUB Group confirms the current, applicable provisions and required documentation for your specific matter at the time of engagement, and does not rely on unverified secondary sources when advising your Society.
