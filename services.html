Our Services

Comprehensive Housing Documentation & Redevelopment Support

From deemed conveyance to full redevelopment project management, D-HUB Group offers structured, transparent consultancy for Co-operative Housing Societies, developers and individual members across Maharashtra.

01

Deemed Conveyance under MOFA

We assist Co-operative Housing Societies and Apartment Associations in obtaining deemed conveyance from promoters/developers who have failed to execute conveyance, under the Maharashtra Ownership Flats Act, 1963 (MOFA) and the Deemed Conveyance Rules, 2019.

  • Title and chain-of-documents verification
  • Drafting and filing Form VII / Form II applications before the Competent Authority (District Deputy Registrar)
  • Representation at hearings before the Competent Authority
  • Liaison for stamping and registration of the deemed conveyance order
  • Advisory on unilateral deemed conveyance where the promoter is untraceable or non-cooperative
02

CHS Redevelopment Consultancy

End-to-end advisory for societies considering self-redevelopment or developer-led redevelopment, protecting members' interests at every stage.

  • Feasibility study and preliminary FSI/TDR assessment (in coordination with architects/liaison consultants)
  • Preparation of tender documents and evaluation criteria for developer selection
  • Vetting of Development Agreements, Permanent Alternate Accommodation Agreements (PAAA) and Power of Attorney
  • General body resolution drafting and compliance with Section 79A directives and Model Bye-laws
  • Corpus, rent and additional-area negotiation support
03

Project Management Consultancy (PMC)

Independent, society-appointed project management to monitor redevelopment construction and safeguard timelines, quality and compliance.

  • Milestone-based construction progress monitoring on behalf of the society
  • Coordination between the society, developer, architect and RCC consultant
  • Verification of statutory approvals — IOD, Commencement Certificate and Occupation/Completion Certificate
  • Periodic reporting to the Managing Committee and general body
  • Support at project handover, including snag-list review and documentation closure
04

Society Formation, Registration & Documentation

Assistance for new and existing societies with registration, statutory records and day-to-day documentation compliance.

  • Co-operative Housing Society registration under the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960
  • Conveyance deed and share certificate documentation
  • Bye-law amendments and Managing Committee election compliance
  • Maintenance of statutory registers and annual filing support
05

Legal & Liaison Support

Coordination with advocates, registered valuers, architects and government offices so that societies have a single point of accountability for their documentation matters.

  • Liaison with the Sub-Registrar's office, Competent Authority and Co-operative Registrar
  • Coordination with panel advocates for litigation arising from conveyance or redevelopment disputes
  • Due diligence support for title, encumbrance and property tax records
Please note: Statutory provisions, Government Resolutions and procedural requirements referred to above are subject to periodic amendment. D-HUB Group confirms the current, applicable provisions and required documentation for your specific matter at the time of engagement, and does not rely on unverified secondary sources when advising your society.
