Our Fees & Engagement Structure

Indicative ranges only — to help you plan and budget, not a binding quotation.

Please read before relying on any figure below: The ranges on this page are indicative only, meant to help you budget and plan. The final fee for your Society's or your specific matter is confirmed in writing by the D-HUB team only after we have reviewed your actual documents, case history and requirements. Depending on the criticality of the documentation and the complexity of the project, the final fee may be higher or lower than the range shown here.

How We Charge

Our fee is paid solely by the party that appoints us — a Society, or a builder, developer or institution. On a Society-appointed engagement, we do not receive any commission, fee or benefit from the developer, contractor or vendor on that same project. Fees are typically structured in one of these ways, depending on the service:

  • Fixed professional fee for a defined scope (e.g., a deemed conveyance matter, a feasibility study), payable in instalments linked to milestones
  • Per-session or per-matter fee for advisory services (e.g., Second Opinion Consultancy, Resolution Drafting)
  • Retainer or percentage-based fee for ongoing PMC engagements spanning the full redevelopment or construction lifecycle, agreed against the specific project's scale and duration

We provide a written fee proposal before any engagement begins, so your Society's General Body or your own decision is made with full clarity.
