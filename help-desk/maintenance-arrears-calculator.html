Interest ranges (up to 3)

Each range is a run of months with one rate of interest a year. Range 2 starts the month after Range 1 ends, and Range 3 starts the month after Range 2 ends.

How interest is calculated. Add monthly maintenance to the opening balance, then multiply by the annual interest rate and divide by 12. Round the interest to the nearest paisa and add it to the balance carried into the next month.

For a month with a ₹1,000 opening balance, ₹500 monthly maintenance, and a 12% annual rate, interest is ₹15 and the closing balance is ₹1,515.

How the figures are worked out
  • Each month: opening balance + monthly maintenance = amount carried in. Interest for the month = amount carried in × annual rate ÷ 12. Closing total = amount carried in + interest.
  • The closing total becomes the next month's opening balance. Interest is therefore charged on the running total, so interest also earns interest. Check this method against your society's bye-laws.
  • Ranges run one after another. Monthly maintenance is added in every month of every range, and each month uses the rate of its own range.
  • Amounts are worked out to the paisa. Interest is rounded to the nearest paisa each month.
