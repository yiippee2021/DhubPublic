Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

1. What This Policy Covers

This policy explains how D-HUB Group (comprising D-HUB Project Management Consultancy and D-Future Hub LLP) handles personal information submitted through the forms on this website (dhubgroup.in) — the Contact Us form and the Society Help Desk form. It does not cover information you share with us by phone, WhatsApp, e-mail, or in person, which is governed by our general professional confidentiality practice as your appointed consultant.

2. What We Collect

Depending on which form you use, we collect:

  • Contact Us form: your name, e-mail address, phone number (optional), society/organisation name (optional), the service you are enquiring about, and your message.
  • Society Help Desk form: your Society's name, location, your role, the topic and your question, your preferred mode of reply, your mobile number, your e-mail address (optional), and an optional photo of a notice or letter you choose to attach.

We do not use cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party trackers on this website, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone browsing without submitting a form.

3. How Your Submission Is Handled

When you submit either form, the information (including any attached photo) is sent directly to our office e-mail address. It is not written to, or stored in, any database on this website or its hosting server — there is nothing for us to "store online" beyond the e-mail itself, which is retained in our office mailbox like any other business correspondence.

A hidden field on both forms helps us silently discard automated spam submissions; it does not collect any information about genuine visitors.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry or question, by phone, WhatsApp, e-mail, or in writing, as you indicate you prefer
  • To assess whether we can assist with your Society's or your personal matter
  • To maintain a record of correspondence with prospective and existing clients, as any professional consultancy does

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third party for marketing purposes. We may share information with a co-professional (for example, an advocate or architect on our team) strictly where necessary to answer your specific query, and only with your knowledge.

5. Your Rights

You may ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you arising from your website submissions, to correct it, or to have it deleted from our records, by writing to admin@dhubgroup.in. We will act on such requests promptly, consistent with our obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and any professional record-keeping requirements that may apply to an ongoing engagement.

6. Photo Uploads

If you attach a photo of a notice or letter on the Society Help Desk form, it is transmitted directly as part of the e-mail sent to our office and is not stored separately on the website or its server. Please avoid attaching documents containing other members' personal or financial information beyond what is necessary for your question.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services or this website change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

8. Contact Us
