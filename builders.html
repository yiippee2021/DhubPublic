For Builders, Developers & Institutions

PMC & Design Consultancy for New Construction

Alongside our Society-appointed redevelopment work, D-HUB Group is engaged directly by builders, developers and institutions as a project management and design consultancy for new construction — first-time residential development, commercial buildings, retail and mall projects, and institutional buildings such as schools and colleges. On this engagement, we work for you, executing the project to schedule, budget and statutory compliance. If you are a Co-operative Housing Society, see our redevelopment and deemed conveyance services instead.

What We Bring

A Single-Window Team for Design, Approvals & Execution

01

Single Point of Accountability

One integrated team for design coordination, statutory approvals and construction monitoring, in place of separate consultants across different firms.

02

Regulatory Fluency

Working knowledge of DCPR 2034, UDCPR 2020 and MahaRERA compliance, kept current through our founder's editorial work at Housing & Property Times.

03

Transparent, Documented Reporting

Milestone-based progress reports and documented approvals, so your project team has a clear record at every stage.

Our Services

PMC & Design Consultancy for Your Development

01

Design & Feasibility Consultancy

FSI/TDR computation, layout planning and development-potential assessment for first-time and greenfield sites, supporting your project's initial design and investment decisions.

02

Statutory Approvals & Plan Sanction

Liaison with MBMC, TMC and BMC for plan sanction, Intimation of Disapproval (IOD), Commencement Certificate and Occupation/Completion Certificate.

03

Project Management Consultancy (Construction)

Progress monitoring, contractor and vendor coordination, and cost, quality and timeline control from groundbreaking through to handover.

04

MahaRERA Registration & Compliance

Project registration, ongoing compliance monitoring, and regulatory documentation under the Maharashtra Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016.

05

Structural Audit & Technical Due Diligence

Independent structural assessments and technical reports supporting design decisions, renovation planning or new-build technical due diligence.

06

Legal & Contract Documentation

Review of construction agreements and vendor/contractor contracts, and preparation of regulatory compliance documentation.

07

Institutional & Commercial Project Advisory

Tailored PMC and design consultancy for malls, retail developments, schools, colleges and other institutional buildings, from concept through to statutory handover.

Regulatory Expertise

Frameworks We Practise Under

DCPR 2034 (BMC jurisdiction) UDCPR 2020 (MBMC / TMC) MahaRERA Act, 2016 MCS Act, 1960 Revenue Records (7/12)
Our mandate on builder and developer engagements: When appointed directly by a builder, developer or institution, D-HUB Group acts for that client to design, secure approvals for, and execute the project. This is a separate engagement from our Society-appointed redevelopment work; the two are never combined on the same project. Statutory provisions referred to above are subject to periodic amendment, and D-HUB Group confirms the current, applicable requirements at the time of engagement.
